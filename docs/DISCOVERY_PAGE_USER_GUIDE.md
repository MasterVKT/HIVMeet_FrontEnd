## 📱 Welcome to Discovery

The **Discovery page** is where you'll meet new people in the HIVMeet community! Swipe through profiles, find matches, and start meaningful conversations in a safe, stigma-free environment.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Accessing Discovery

1. **Open the HIVMeet app** and log in
2. **Tap the Discovery icon** (🔥) in the bottom navigation bar
3. **Start swiping!** Profiles will appear one at a time

**First-time users:** You'll be prompted to enable location services for accurate distance calculations. This is optional but recommended for the best experience.

---

## ❤️ How to Swipe

### Swipe Gestures

| Gesture | Action | What Happens |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Swipe Right →** | Like | You like this profile. If they like you back, it's a match! |
| **Swipe Left ←** | Pass | You're not interested. This profile won't be shown again. |
| **Swipe Up ↑** | Super Like | ⭐ **Premium Only** - Send a special like that notifies the other user immediately. |

### Action Buttons

Prefer tapping instead of swiping? Use the action buttons:

- **❌ Red X Button:** Pass (same as swipe left)
- **💚 Green Heart Button:** Like (same as swipe right)
- **⭐ Gold Star Button:** Super Like (same as swipe up, premium only)

**Accessibility Tip:** Action buttons are fully compatible with screen readers (TalkBack, VoiceOver) and provide an alternative to swipe gestures.

## 📸 Viewing Profiles

### Profile Information Displayed

Each profile card shows:

- **Photos** - Swipe left/right on the photo carousel (dots show pagination)
- **Name & Age** - e.g., "Sarah, 28"
- **Distance** - How far away they are (e.g., "5.2 km")
- **Compatibility Score** - Percentage match based on your preferences (e.g., "85%")
- **Bio** - Short description (tap to expand)
- **Interests** - Shared interests highlighted (e.g., "Travel 🌍", "Photography 📷")

### Profile Badges

Look for these special badges:

- **✓ Verified** - Profile has been verified by HIVMeet
- **⭐ Premium** - Premium subscriber
- **🟢 Online Now** - Currently active on the app
- **Last Active** - "Active 2 hours ago" (if not online)

### Viewing Full Profile

**Tap anywhere on the profile card** to open the detailed profile view:

- See all photos in full-screen carousel
- Read complete bio
- View all interests and preferences
- Check compatibility breakdown
- See mutual friends (if any)

**Tip:** In profile detail view, you can still like or pass using the bottom action buttons.

## 🎉 When You Get a Match

### It's a Match!

When you and another user both like each other, you'll see:

1. **Match Animation** - Celebratory animation with confetti 🎊
2. **Both Profile Photos** - Side-by-side display
3. **"It's a Match!" Message** - In your selected language

### What to Do Next

Two options appear:

- **💬 Send Message** - Opens a conversation with your match immediately
- **🔥 Keep Swiping** - Closes the modal and continues Discovery

**You can message your match anytime** from the **Matches** tab (💕 icon in bottom navigation).

## 🔍 Customizing Your Discovery Filters

### Accessing Filters

1. Tap the **Filter icon** (⚙️ or filter funnel) in the top-right corner of Discovery
2. Adjust your preferences
3. Tap **Apply Filters**
4. Discovery will reload with profiles matching your new criteria

### Available Filters

#### Age Range
- **What it does:** Shows only profiles within your selected age range
- **Range:** 18 to 99 years
- **How to use:** Drag the double slider to set minimum and maximum age
- **Example:** 25-35 shows profiles aged 25 to 35

#### Distance
- **What it does:** Shows only profiles within X kilometers from you
- **Range:** 1 to 100 km
- **How to use:** Drag the slider to set maximum distance
- **Example:** 50 km shows profiles up to 50 kilometers away

#### Relationship Type
- **What it does:** Filter by what people are looking for
- **Options:**
  - Long-term relationship
  - Casual dating
  - Friendship
  - Not sure yet
- **How to use:** Tap to select multiple options (checkmarks appear)

#### Interests
- **What it does:** Show profiles with specific interests
- **Limit:** Select up to 5 interests
- **How to use:** Tap interests to select (e.g., Travel, Sports, Music)
- **Tip:** Profiles with mutual interests appear higher in your stack

#### Premium Filters ⭐

**Verified Profiles Only** (Premium)
- **What it does:** Shows only verified users
- **Toggle:** ON = verified only, OFF = all profiles

**Online Only** (Premium)
- **What it does:** Shows only users currently online
- **Toggle:** ON = online users only, OFF = all profiles

**Upgrade to unlock:** Tap the upgrade button in filters to access premium features.

### Real-Time Profile Count

At the bottom of the Filters page, you'll see:

**"~150 profiles match your filters"**

This updates in real-time as you adjust filters, helping you find the right balance between specificity and selection.

### Resetting Filters

Tap **Clear Filters** to reset all filters to default settings:
- Age: 18-99
- Distance: 100 km
- All relationship types included
- No specific interests selected

## ⭐ Premium Features

### Super Like

**What it does:**
- Sends a special like notification to the other user
- Your profile appears highlighted in their Discovery
- Increases match chances by ~3x

**How to use:**
1. Swipe up on a profile (or tap the ⭐ gold star button)
2. Confirmation dialog appears: "Send a Super Like?"
3. Tap "Send" to confirm
4. Distinctive star animation plays

**Availability:**
- **Free Users:** 1 per week
- **Premium Users:** 5 per day

### Rewind

**What it does:** Undo your last swipe (like or pass)

**How to use:**
1. After swiping, tap the **↩️ Rewind button** (top-left)
2. Previous profile returns to your stack
3. You can change your decision

**Availability:** Premium only

**Limitations:**
- Can only rewind the most recent swipe
- Cannot rewind after a match (match is final)
- Cannot rewind after app restart

### Boost

**What it does:** Your profile appears as a top profile for 30 minutes

**How to use:** Available in **Profile → Settings → Boost**

**Results:**
- Up to 10x more profile views
- Significantly higher match rate during boost period

**Availability:** Premium only (3 boosts per month)

- **iOS:** VoiceOver fully supported

- Navigate through profiles with swipe gestures or tap actions

#### Large Text Support
- Discovery page supports **up to 200% text scaling**
- Go to your device settings → Accessibility → Display Size & Text
- All text remains readable and layouts adapt

#### High Contrast Mode
- Automatic support for system-wide high contrast mode
- All text meets **WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards** (4.5:1 ratio minimum)

#### Reduced Motion
- If you enable "Reduce Motion" in device settings:
  - Swipe animations are simplified
  - Match modal animation is reduced
  - App respects your preference across all screens

#### Touch Targets
- All buttons are **at least 44x44 dp** (WCAG minimum)
- Preferred size: **56x56 dp** for easier tapping
- Sufficient spacing between buttons to prevent accidental taps

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How does the algorithm work?**
A: Discovery shows profiles based on:
1. Your filters (age, distance, interests)
2. Compatibility score (calculated from preferences)
3. Recent activity (active users appear higher)
4. Verified profiles (boosted slightly)

**Q: Why do I see the same profiles sometimes?**
A: If a user updates their profile or photos, they may reappear in your Discovery. Additionally, if you've exhausted all matching profiles, the app may show previously passed profiles after 30 days.

**Q: Can someone see if I've viewed their profile?**
A: No. Profile views are completely private. Users only know you liked them if you swipe right or super like.

**Q: How accurate is the distance?**
A: Distance is calculated from your current GPS location to theirs. Accuracy depends on:
- Location permissions enabled
- GPS signal strength
- Last known location (if user is offline)

### Matching Questions

**Q: If I accidentally passed someone, can I find them again?**
A: **Premium users** can use the **Rewind** feature (↩️) to undo the last swipe. Free users cannot undo accidental swipes.

**Q: What happens after I match?**
A: You'll see a match modal. You can:
1. Send a message immediately (opens conversation)
2. Keep swiping (find them later in Matches tab 💕)

Matches never expire unless you unmatch.

**Q: Can I unmatch someone?**
A: Yes. Go to **Matches → Select Match → Menu (⋮) → Unmatch**. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

### Privacy & Safety

**Q: Who can see my profile?**
A: Only users who:
- Match your discovery filters (age, distance, etc.)
- You match their filters
- Are within 100 km of your location

**Q: Can I hide my profile temporarily?**
A: Yes. Go to **Profile → Settings → Privacy → Pause Discovery**. Your profile won't appear in others' Discovery, but existing matches remain.

**Q: How do I report or block someone?**
A:
- **Report:** Tap profile → Menu (⋮) → Report User (select reason)
- **Block:** Tap profile → Menu (⋮) → Block User (immediate, no notification sent)

Blocked users cannot see your profile or message you.

**Q: Is my HIV status visible to others?**
A: **No.** Your HIV status is private. You choose when and how to disclose this information in conversations or your bio.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why isn't Discovery loading?**
A: Try these steps:
1. Check internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
2. Pull down to refresh the page
3. Close and reopen the app
4. Check if filters are too restrictive (try adjusting)
5. Ensure location permissions are enabled

**Q: Why are photos loading slowly?**
A: This can happen with slower connections. Photos are optimized for mobile, but load time depends on your network speed. Try switching to Wi-Fi for faster loading.

**Q: The app says "No more profiles available." Why?**
A: Possible reasons:
- Your filters are too restrictive → Try adjusting age range or distance
- You've seen all matching profiles in your area → Check back later
- Location is too remote → Increase distance filter

**Q: Does Discovery work offline?**
A: No. Discovery requires an active internet connection to load profiles. However, profiles you've already seen are cached and can be viewed offline in your Matches.

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Optimize Your Profile
- **Upload 4-6 high-quality photos** (profiles with 5+ photos get 3x more matches)
- **Write a genuine bio** (profiles with bios get 40% more likes)
- **Be honest about your interests** (mutual interests increase compatibility score)
- **Get verified** (verified profiles appear higher in Discovery)

### Swiping Strategy
- **Be selective but open-minded** - Quality over quantity
- **Read full bios** - Tap to view profile details before deciding
- **Use Super Likes wisely** - Save them for profiles you're truly interested in
- **Swipe consistently** - Active users appear higher in others' Discovery

### Conversation Starters
After a match:
- **Comment on mutual interests** - "I saw you love hiking! What's your favorite trail?"
- **Ask open-ended questions** - Avoid yes/no questions
- **Be respectful and genuine** - HIVMeet is a community built on empathy
- **Don't rush** - Take time to get to know your matches

### Safety First
- **Meet in public places** for first dates
- **Tell a friend** where you're going
- **Trust your instincts** - If something feels off, unmatch or report
- **Don't share personal info too quickly** (phone number, address, etc.)
- **Report suspicious behavior** - Help keep the community safe

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Issue:** "Connection error" message appears

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data
3. Tap "Retry" button
4. Force close and reopen app

---

**Issue:** Swipe gestures not working

**Solutions:**
1. Try using action buttons (❌ or 💚) instead
2. Restart the app
3. Check if device touch screen is working properly
4. Ensure no screen protector is interfering

---

**Issue:** Daily limit reached but counter shows remaining likes

**Solutions:**
- Limit resets at midnight (your timezone)
- Pull down to refresh the Discovery page
- Log out and log back in to sync data

---

**Issue:** Match modal stuck on screen

**Solutions:**
1. Tap outside the modal or tap "Keep Swiping"
2. Use device back button (Android) or swipe down (iOS)
3. If stuck, force close app and reopen

---

**Issue:** Verified/Online filters not working

**Solutions:**
- These are **premium-only features**
- Upgrade to Premium to unlock
- If already premium, log out and log back in to refresh subscription status
